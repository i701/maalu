# Budget and goals (API and its web side)

- The budget is simple limits, not envelopes: a line is a per-period amount for some categories (each category in one line at most). **A limit can also be for set dates** (`budget_lines.ends_on`, with `start_date` as its first day; `start_date` + `end_date` on create and update, both or neither, end not before start, at most ten years, no rollover): its `amount` is then **one total for the whole stretch**, counted once over `start_date`..`ends_on` (`BudgetService._fixed_view`, the same activity rules as any line), not per period. **Fixed limits are separate from the per-period lines**: they are returned in `fixed_lines` (every one, whatever period is shown, each with `state` upcoming / active / ended and `days_left`; running ones first, ended last), they are **not in `lines` or `totals`**, and `attention` counts a running one that is close or over. The "each category in one line" rule only applies **within a kind**: two per-period lines can't share a category, two limits for set dates can't share one when their dates overlap (`_overlap`; the ends count), but a fixed limit and a per-period one may. A PATCH can give a per-period limit both dates (it becomes a fixed one, rollover off), change one end of a fixed one, or send `end_date: null` to make it per period again (`start_date` is then reset to the current period; a lone date on a per-period limit is a 422). Notifications and the save-time warning treat a **running** fixed limit like a line of the current period (`budget:<line id>:<start_date>:<status>`; the web warning only fires when the expense's date is inside the dates, even when another period is on screen: `budgetWarning(budget, categories, on)`). Web: the limit form's "Only for certain dates" switch (From and Until, the amount relabelled "Limit for these dates", rollover hidden, categories taken by an overlapping limit disabled), a "For set dates" section on the Budget page under the per-period cards (each card shows its dates and days left), not in the summary above it. **The API returns every one, but the page lists only those whose dates touch the period on screen** (`fixedInPeriod` in `lib/budget.ts`), so a November limit is not shown under December; the form still reads the full list to tell which categories are taken. **A monthly limit can run only between two dates** (`budget_lines.active_until`, `BudgetLineCreate.repeat`, `BudgetLineUpdate.repeat`): with both dates and `repeat: true` the limit stays per-period (`ends_on` null, `amount` is per period, rollover allowed) but only exists in the periods the dates touch; **the dates snap to whole periods** (`start_date` = first day of the period holding the start, `active_until` = last day of the one holding the end) and the response says so (`runs_from`, `runs_until`, null for one that runs for ever). `view()` leaves it out of `lines`, `totals`, `attention` and the notifications outside its periods (`_runs_in`). Overlap is by kind and days (`_span_of`, `_overlap`): a fixed limit only meets fixed ones, a monthly one meets monthly ones in any period both run in (so Nov to Jan and Feb to Apr may share a category, a monthly one that runs for ever clashes with both); the web form leaves that check to the server for these. A PATCH with the dates keeps the limit's kind unless `repeat` says otherwise (true: monthly between dates, false: one total; moves of a limit that stops being monthly go, as before); `end_date: null` makes it run for every period again (start reset to the current period). **A move needs both limits to run in the period it is for** (422 otherwise). Web: the form's "How it counts" select under the dates, "Runs …" on the card. **Rolling a finished (or running) fixed limit over**: on the web, "Repeat for the next stretch" on its card opens `BudgetLineForm` as a new limit (`repeatOf`) filled in with the same name, categories and amount, starting the day after and as long (`nextStretch`), and saves through the normal create; iOS does the same from the limit's own screen (`BudgetLineDraft(repeating:)`), and its limit form has the same dates switch, From and Until, and "How it counts". **The page lists a fixed limit only under the period its dates touch** (`fixedInPeriod`), and **the dashboard's Budget and goals card lists the running ones** with a meter (`runningFixed`), even when there are no per-period limits. Not built: a fixed limit's own history across stretches. Periods are months starting on `period_start_day` (default 1), derived in `app/budget_period.py`, never stored. Activity is expenses in `base_amount` (split items under their own categories); status is `ok` / `warning` (at or above the warning percent, default 80, per line or per budget) / `over`. Rollover carries unspent money only, looking back at most 24 periods from the line's `start_date`. **Moving money between limits** (`models/budget.py: BudgetMove`, table `budget_moves`; `POST /budget/moves {to_line_id, from_line_id?, amount, on?, note?}` and `POST /budget/moves/{id}/undo`, both answering with the budget for that period; `GET /budget/lines/{id}/moves` is the limit's **history**): money moved from one monthly limit to another for **one period only**, or added to one (no `from_line_id`, a top-up, which raises `totals.amount`; a move between limits leaves it as is). A line's `amount` stays its usual amount; `moved_in` and `moved_out` are this period's, and `available`, `percent`, `status`, the totals and rollover (each earlier period's moves, through `net` in `view`) all use amount + carried + moved_in - moved_out. **A source gives at most what it has left that period** (422 "has only MVR X left" / "has nothing left"); a top-up has no cap. Only monthly limits take part: a limit for set dates is refused (422), and giving a monthly limit dates deletes its moves; deleting a limit deletes them too (`ON DELETE CASCADE`). A move stores its period's first day and is matched to periods **by date range**, so changing `period_start_day` later still finds it. `BudgetResponse.moves` lists the period's moves, newest first. **Undo never deletes**: it sets `undone_at`, the budget ignores undone moves (`_moves`), and the history keeps them, so it is a log of what moved where (a second undo is 404). The history (`BudgetMoveLogEntry`: the move plus its `period_start`, `period_end` under the current start day, and `undone_at`) lists every move into or out of the limit, in every period, newest first. Moves are in backups (an owned table). Web: `MoveMoneyDialog` ("Move money here" on each monthly card; From lists the other limits with what they have left, most first, an empty one disabled, plus "Nowhere: just add to this limit"; `moveProblem` checks the cap in the browser too), each card lists its moves (`movesFor`: "+MVR 600.00 from Dining this period", "−MVR 600.00 to Groceries", "+MVR 300.00 added") with Undo, a **History** button opens `MoveHistoryDialog` (grouped by period with `historyByPeriod`, each move with its note, when it was made and, struck through with an Undone chip, when it was undone), and `periodLimit` is the card's "of" figure (`lib/budget-moves.ts`).
- Goals (`/goals`) are savings targets in the base currency. A goal either follows one savings account's balance (one goal per account; cards and loans refused) or, with no account, a log of amounts set aside by hand (`goal_entries`, signed). An optional monthly plan reports what was set aside in a budget period: for an account goal, transfers into the account less transfers out (income and interest are not contributions). `needed_per_month` is the remaining amount over the whole months left, rounded up.
