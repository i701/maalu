# Third-party designs

- **Bencho** (<https://bencho.dev>, MIT licence, <https://bencho.dev/licence>): the design of the Context meter, Traffic (stacked bars), Asset swap and One-time code blocks, redrawn in SwiftUI for the iOS app rather than copied as code. Used so far: the Context meter's cut bar, chips, rolling figure and colour palette (`apps/ios/Maalu/Features/Dashboard/Meter/`, `Shared/Design/MeterPalette.swift`), Traffic's stacked bars (`apps/ios/Maalu/Features/Dashboard/Trend/`), and Asset swap's two slabs, turning arrow and slab-grows-into-a-list picker (`apps/ios/Maalu/Features/Transactions/Form/Transfer/`), and One-time code's cells, digits rising in, caret, shiver and the cells fusing into a "Verified" capsule (`apps/ios/Maalu/Features/Login/Otp/`, and `apps/web/src/components/OtpField.tsx` on the web, redrawn in React with MUI tokens and the project's own icon instead of lucide).
- **Topography pattern** (`apps/ios/Maalu/Resources/Assets.xcassets/TopographyPattern.imageset/topography.svg`), the line pattern behind the Dashboard's deck cards. It came from a pattern generator that looks like Hero Patterns (heropatterns.com, patterns by Steve Schoger, CC BY 4.0, which asks for attribution). **Confirm where it was downloaded and keep the credit that site's licence asks for.**
